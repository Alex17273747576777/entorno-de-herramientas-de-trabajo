## ❓ Preguntas del estudio

**¿Para qué leo en el PC si tengo el móvil?**
Colecciones largas, PDF enormes, EPUB con notas, cómics a doble página: la pantalla grande no compite con el bolsillo — lo completa. Y los servidores de la bodega convierten el escritorio en la sala de lectura de toda la casa.

**¿Y las fuentes de dónde salen?**
Como siempre en esta casa: cada lector con su geografía — LNReader alimenta a IReader, FMD2 baja para Yomikiru y compañía, Paperback bebe de Komga. La pieza de extensiones (en camino) cartografiará ese río.

## The study's rule

On the desktop the stick changes nuance: the ink readers (KOReader, Calibre — piece 36) already have their thread; this is the corner of those who process. And the day's lesson: **the trade gets verified, not assumed** — koharu looked like a reader and is a translator; it gets listed by what it does, not by what the name promises.

## Why the nuance matters

A page translator and a reader share a shelf in the directories — but their houses, their releases and their care differ. The map that confuses trades ends up recommending the wrong tool with total confidence.

**The method:** open the house → read what the project says about itself → compare with what the catalog said about it → list the REAL trade with a date. If the catalog lied, the record says so — no drama.

**Disclosure:** a small project among friends, nothing commercial — catalogs are a source of leads, never of verdicts.
