HELP & SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I safely download the official Android APK?

You can download the official PlayTorrio APK directly from the Downloads section above or our official GitHub Releases. We provide ARM64-v8a (modern phones, tablets, and TV) and 32-bit armeabi-v7a. On Fire TV, enter Downloader code 4054777.

PlayTorrio not working, crashing, or stream won't load?

First, verify you have installed the latest version. If a specific scraper or channel is slow, try an alternative source or connect a Real-Debrid / TorBox account in Settings for uncapped cloud caching. For live troubleshooting, bug reports, and assistance, join our active Discord Community.

Can I use Stremio addons in PlayTorrio?

Yes! PlayTorrio features a built-in Stremio addons engine. You can install Torrentio, Comet, CyberFlix, and custom catalog providers directly within the app.

How does torrent streaming work without downloading?

PlayTorrio streams torrents sequentially. Rather than waiting for the entire file to download, the built-in torrent engine downloads data pieces in order so you can start watching movies and series instantly with hardware acceleration.

Can I watch free anime and read manga?

Yes. PlayTorrio features a dedicated anime streaming hub with AniList syncing, multiple scraper servers, English/Arabic subtitles and dubs, plus a high-res manga and comic reader.

Is PlayTorrio completely free?

Yes. PlayTorrio is 100% free and open-source. There are no subscriptions, paywalls, or hidden charges.

Do I need a Real-Debrid account?

No. PlayTorrio works out of the box with standard torrent and direct scrapers. Connecting Real-Debrid, AllDebrid, Premiumize, or TorBox gives you faster uncapped cloud streams.

How do I install on Fire TV or Android TV?

Install the Downloader app on your TV, enter code 4054777 (or 8356029 for older devices), and click Go to download and install.

How do I turn off Discord Rich Presence?

Go to Settings in PlayTorrio and switch off Discord Rich Presence. Your status will immediately stop broadcasting.

macOS says "App is damaged". How do I fix it?

Open Terminal on your Mac and run: xattr -cr /Applications/PlayTorrio.app. This clears Apple Gatekeeper's unsigned app quarantine flag.
